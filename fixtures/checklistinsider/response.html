Sports Card Release Calendar

Our sports card release calendar helps you stay informed about the latest card products. Listings are grouped by date, and there is also a true calendar view so that you can plan out your month.

The New Releases tab covers the sets available in the current month and future months, while all earlier sets are displayed on the Older Releases tab.

By default, the new release calendar lists products by date, with the most recent at the top and the oldest at the bottom. These can be further filtered by sport using the dropdown menu. Click on the "Choose Category" menu to focus on one specific sport. Collectors can also click the "Calendar View" tab to review releases across specific months.

Each link on the Sports Card Release Calendar takes you to a rundown of the product, with checklists, set details, box breakdowns and more, as available, for various Panini, Topps and Upper Deck sets.

Previously, most trading card sets were announced well in advance, typically around six months before the release date. In the current hobby, the timeline is much shorter, especially for Topps and Panini. Some sets are not officially revealed until weeks or even days before the official release. In fact, Topps frequently announces sets about a month (or less) before the actual release, as part of the presale on the Topps website. While Upper Deck has remained consistent in providing set details in advance, the final release dates are only confirmed about a month or two out.

Review recent and upcoming card releases for various sports, including baseball, basketball, football, golf, hockey, MMA, racing, soccer, tennis, wrestling, and non-sport, using the Sports Card Release Calendar below.

You can also go directly to the main hubs, which feature all sets available on the site.
