30 Years of Pokémon TCG Evolution
1999
Charizard
Base Set
Charizard’s Fire Spin attack was the highest-damage attack in the entire
Base Set
expansion, setting a trend for Charizard’s frequently massive damage output in the Pokémon TCG.
2000
Erika’s Jigglypuff
Gym Challenge
Trainers never pulled their punches while battling with Erika’s Jigglypuff. Attach a Double Colorless Energy and play a few PlusPower, and you could KO most Basic Pokémon in the game. This adorable Jigglypuff was one of the first Trainer’s Pokémon, a kind of card that originally appeared in the
Gym Heroes
expansion.
2005
Scizor ex
EX Unseen Forces
You might be familiar with modern Pokémon ex, but this kind of card first debuted in 2003 as part of the
EX
Series. Scizor ex was unique because it was one of the only Pokémon ex that could evolve from two different Pokémon—Scyther
or
Scyther ex.
2011
N
Black & White—Noble Victories
Supporter cards continue to boast powerful effects, but N remains one of the most memorable comeback cards of all time. Not only that, but this ultra rare version from the
Black & White—Noble Victories
expansion was the first-ever full-art Supporter card.
2016
Greninja BREAK
XY—BREAKpoint
Pokémon BREAK were a unique kind of card that evolved from otherwise fully evolved Pokémon, allowing them to use all the attacks and Abilities of the Pokémon they evolved from. Greninja BREAK had the powerful Giant Water Shuriken Ability, and it saw true success when paired with Greninja from the
XY—BREAKpoint
expansion, which had the ever-popular Shadow Stitching attack.
2019
Pikachu & Zekrom-
GX
Sun & Moon—Team Up
Two words: big Basic. TAG TEAM Pokémon-
GX
were the first-ever Pokémon cards to yield three Prize cards after being Knocked Out. High HP, powerful attacks, and fantastic illustrations featuring dynamic duos made cards like Pikachu & Zekrom-
GX
especially popular. Using Tag Bolt-
GX
to take six Prize cards in one turn was one of the coolest feelings of the era.
2023
Magikarp
Scarlet & Violet—Paldea Evolved
Illustration rare cards debuted in the
Scarlet & Violet
expansion, and they’ve been fan favorites ever since. Shinji Kanda’s detailed illustration of Magikarp pushing past the trial of a waterfall is everything fans want and more. Did you notice Gyarados is in the artwork too?
2026
Mew ex
